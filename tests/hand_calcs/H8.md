# H8 — high-income couple, salaries 9,000,000 (40) and 7,000,000 (38), child 5

**Earner A (9,000,000, age 40 → LTC).** Monthly 750,000 → band 730,000–770,000 → standard 750,000 (grade 38).
Health+LTC 43,012.5 → 43,012 × 12 = 516,144; levy 862.5 → 862 × 12 = 10,344; pension capped at standard monthly pay 650,000 (厚生年金 grade 32; health grade 35): 59,475 × 12 = 713,700; employment 3,750 × 12 = 45,000 → **1,285,188**.
Employment income: over 8,500,000 → 9,000,000 − 1,950,000 = 7,050,000. Income-adjustment deduction (child under 23): (9,000,000 − 8,500,000) × 10% = 50,000 → total income **7,000,000**.
Income tax: basic 620,000 (6.55m–23.5m). 7,000,000 − 620,000 − 1,285,188 = 5,094,812 → 5,094,000 × 20% − 427,500 = 591,300 × 1.021 = 603,717.3 → **603,700**.
Resident: 7,000,000 − 430,000 − 1,285,188 = 5,284,812 → 5,284,000. 特別区民税 317,040 − 1,500 (3% of the minimum 50,000) = 315,540 → 315,500 + 3,000 = 318,500; 都民税 211,360 − 1,000 = 210,360 → 210,300 + 1,000 = 211,300; forest 1,000 → **530,800**.

**Earner B (7,000,000, age 38).** Monthly 583,333.33 → band 575,000–605,000 → standard 590,000 (grade 33).
Health 29,057.5 → 29,057 × 12 = 348,684; levy 678.5 → 678 × 12 = 8,136; pension 53,985 × 12 = 647,820; employment 2,916.67 → 2,917 × 12 = 35,004 → **1,039,644**.
Employment income: 7,000,000 − (7,000,000 × 10% + 1,100,000) = **5,200,000** (no adjustment deduction: salary ≤ 8.5m).
Income tax: basic 670,000 (4.89m–6.55m). 5,200,000 − 670,000 − 1,039,644 = 3,490,356 → 3,490,000 × 20% − 427,500 = 270,500 × 1.021 = 276,180.5 → **276,100**.
Resident: 5,200,000 − 430,000 − 1,039,644 = 3,730,356 → 3,730,000. 特別区民税 223,800 − 1,500 = 222,300 + 3,000 = 225,300; 都民税 149,200 − 1,000 = 148,200 + 1,000 = 149,200; forest 1,000 → **375,500**.

**Child allowance.** 10,000 × 12 = **120,000**.

**Disposable income** = 16,000,000 + 120,000 − (603,700 + 530,800 + 276,100 + 375,500) − (1,285,188 + 1,039,644) = **12,009,068**

**Revision 2026-11-03 (resident-tax rounding, OPEN_QUESTIONS Q14).** 地方税法20条の4の2第3項 rounds each tax's amount down to 100 yen, and 第8項 treats 特別区民税 and 都民税 as one tax only when a whole amount is under 100 yen (sources/law/chihozeiho_20270101.xml). So the income levy is worked out separately: 特別区民税 6% (314条の3) less 3% of the 調整控除 base (314条の6), and 都民税 4% (35条) less 2% (37条), each rounded down to 100 yen, then 均等割 3,000 + 1,000 and the forest environment tax 1,000. Ota City's worked example does the same (sources/tax/ota_keisan_no_shikumi.html: 201,540 → 201,500 and 133,360 → 133,300).
Before this revision earner A's levy was rounded once (525,900 + 5,000 = 530,900), and disposable income was 100 yen lower.
