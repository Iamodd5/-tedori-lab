# H8 — high-income couple, salaries 9,000,000 (40) and 7,000,000 (38), child 5

**Earner A (9,000,000, age 40 → LTC).** Monthly 750,000 → band 730,000–770,000 → standard 750,000 (grade 38).
Health+LTC 43,012.5 → 43,012 × 12 = 516,144; levy 862.5 → 862 × 12 = 10,344; pension capped at grade 35: 59,475 × 12 = 713,700; employment 3,750 × 12 = 45,000 → **1,285,188**.
Employment income: over 8,500,000 → 9,000,000 − 1,950,000 = 7,050,000. Income-adjustment deduction (child under 23): (9,000,000 − 8,500,000) × 10% = 50,000 → total income **7,000,000**.
Income tax: basic 620,000 (6.55m–23.5m). 7,000,000 − 620,000 − 1,285,188 = 5,094,812 → 5,094,000 × 20% − 427,500 = 591,300 × 1.021 = 603,717.3 → **603,700**.
Resident: 7,000,000 − 430,000 − 1,285,188 = 5,284,812 → 5,284,000 × 10% = 528,400 − 2,500 (minimum 調整控除) = 525,900 + 5,000 = **530,900**.

**Earner B (7,000,000, age 38).** Monthly 583,333.33 → band 575,000–605,000 → standard 590,000 (grade 33).
Health 29,057.5 → 29,057 × 12 = 348,684; levy 678.5 → 678 × 12 = 8,136; pension 53,985 × 12 = 647,820; employment 2,916.67 → 2,917 × 12 = 35,004 → **1,039,644**.
Employment income: 7,000,000 − (7,000,000 × 10% + 1,100,000) = **5,200,000** (no adjustment deduction: salary ≤ 8.5m).
Income tax: basic 670,000 (4.89m–6.55m). 5,200,000 − 670,000 − 1,039,644 = 3,490,356 → 3,490,000 × 20% − 427,500 = 270,500 × 1.021 = 276,180.5 → **276,100**.
Resident: 5,200,000 − 430,000 − 1,039,644 = 3,730,356 → 3,730,000 × 10% = 373,000 − 2,500 = 370,500 + 5,000 = **375,500**.

**Child allowance.** 10,000 × 12 = **120,000**.

**Disposable income** = 16,000,000 + 120,000 − (603,700 + 530,900 + 276,100 + 375,500) − (1,285,188 + 1,039,644) = **12,008,968**
