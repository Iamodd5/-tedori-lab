# H5 — single mother, salary 2,500,000 (38), child 10

**Social insurance.** Monthly 208,333.33 → band 195,000–210,000 → standard 200,000 (grade 17).
Health 9,850 × 12 = 118,200; levy 230 × 12 = 2,760; pension 18,300 × 12 = 219,600; employment 1,041.67 → 1,042 × 12 = 12,504 → **353,064**.

**Income.** Salary 2,500,000 is in the table band 2,500,000–2,504,000 → 2,500,000 × 70% − 80,000 = **1,670,000**.

**Income tax.** 1,670,000 − basic 1,040,000 − 353,064 − single-parent 350,000 < 0 → **0**.

**Resident tax.** Total income 1,670,000 > 1,350,000, so the single-parent exemption does not apply.
1,670,000 − 430,000 − 353,064 − 300,000 = 586,936 → 586,000. 調整控除 base: difference = 50,000 + 50,000 = 100,000; taxable ≤ 2m → min(100,000, 586,000) = 100,000.
特別区民税: 586,000 × 6% = 35,160 − 3% × 100,000 = 32,160 → 32,100 + 3,000 = 35,100. 都民税: 586,000 × 4% = 23,440 − 2% × 100,000 = 21,440 → 21,400 + 1,000 = 22,400. Forest environment tax 1,000. Total **58,500**.

**Child allowance.** 10,000 × 12 = **120,000**.

**Disposable income** = 2,500,000 + 120,000 − 58,500 − 353,064 = **2,208,436**

**Revision 2026-11-03 (resident-tax rounding, OPEN_QUESTIONS Q14).** 地方税法20条の4の2第3項 rounds each tax's amount down to 100 yen, and 第8項 treats 特別区民税 and 都民税 as one tax only when a whole amount is under 100 yen (sources/law/chihozeiho_20270101.xml). So the income levy is worked out separately: 特別区民税 6% (314条の3) less 3% of the 調整控除 base (314条の6), and 都民税 4% (35条) less 2% (37条), each rounded down to 100 yen, then 均等割 3,000 + 1,000 and the forest environment tax 1,000. Ota City's worked example does the same (sources/tax/ota_keisan_no_shikumi.html: 201,540 → 201,500 and 133,360 → 133,300).
Before this revision the 10% levy was rounded once: 53,600 + 5,000 = 58,600, disposable 2,208,336.

**Revision 2026-11-03: 児童扶養手当 (child-rearing allowance), OPEN_QUESTIONS Q17.** Sources: 児童扶養手当法 (sources/law/jido_fuyo_teate_ho_336AC0000000238.xml)
and 施行令 (sources/law/jido_fuyo_teate_sekourei_336CO0000000405.xml, in force 2026-04-01).
- Income for the test (施行令4条1項): salary income before any income-adjustment deduction, less 100,000 (給与所得 or 公的年金等), then
  less 80,000: 1,670,000 − 100,000 − 80,000 = **1,490,000**. No 養育費 and none of the 4条2項 deductions (a mother gets no
  ひとり親 deduction here).
- Limits (2条の4): one dependant (the child, under 16) adds 380,000. Full payment below 690,000 + 380,000 = 1,070,000;
  nothing at or above 2,080,000 + 380,000 = 2,460,000. H5 is in between: partial payment.
- Amount from April 2026 (2条の2): 48,050 a month for the first child. Withheld (2条の4第3項): (1,490,000 − 1,070,000) × 0.0264029
  = 11,089.218 → rounded to 10 yen (under 5 down, 5 or more up) = 11,090, plus 10 = 11,100.
  Monthly 48,050 − 11,100 = **36,950**; × 12 = **443,400** (the April 2026 amounts for all 12 months, an approximation).
- It is not taxed (児童扶養手当法25条), so taxes and premiums above are unchanged.

**Disposable income** = 2,208,436 + 443,400 = **2,651,836**

Check against the ministry's minimum: just below the 2,460,000 limit, withheld = round10(1,389,999 × 0.0264029 = 36,700.0) + 10 = 36,710,
so 48,050 − 36,710 = 11,340 a month, the "一部支給：48,040円～11,340円" lower end in こども家庭庁 「児童扶養手当制度の概要」
(sources/child/cfa_20260408_jido_fuyo_teate_gaiyou.pdf, 2026-04-08).
