# H5 — single mother, salary 2,500,000 (38), child 10

**Social insurance.** Monthly 208,333.33 → band 195,000–210,000 → standard 200,000 (grade 17).
Health 9,850 × 12 = 118,200; levy 230 × 12 = 2,760; pension 18,300 × 12 = 219,600; employment 1,041.67 → 1,042 × 12 = 12,504 → **353,064**.

**Income.** Salary 2,500,000 is in the table band 2,500,000–2,504,000 → 2,500,000 × 70% − 80,000 = **1,670,000**.

**Income tax.** 1,670,000 − basic 1,040,000 − 353,064 − single-parent 350,000 < 0 → **0**.

**Resident tax.** Total income 1,670,000 > 1,350,000, so the single-parent exemption does not apply.
1,670,000 − 430,000 − 353,064 − 300,000 = 586,936 → 586,000 × 10% = 58,600. 調整控除: difference = 50,000 + 50,000 = 100,000; taxable ≤ 2m → 5% × min(100,000, 586,000) = 5,000 → 53,600 + 5,000 = **58,600**.

**Child allowance.** 10,000 × 12 = **120,000**.

**Disposable income** = 2,500,000 + 120,000 − 58,600 − 353,064 = **2,208,336**
