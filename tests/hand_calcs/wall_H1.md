# H1 wall map — hand checks (Milestone 4)

Rates and bands: 協会けんぽ Tokyo FY2026 table (parameters/baseline_2026/kyoukaikenpo_tokyo_r8.csv); employee half.

## Salary 1,110,000 → 1,120,000: premium band step
- Monthly pay 92,500 → standard monthly 88,000; 93,333 → standard monthly 98,000.
- Health 9.85% half: 88,000 → 4,334 × 12 = 52,008; 98,000 → 4,826.5 → 4,826 × 12 = 57,912 (+5,904).
- Child levy: 101 × 12 = 1,212 → 113 × 12 = 1,356 (+144).
- Pension 18.3% half: 8,052 × 12 = 96,624 → 8,967 × 12 = 107,604 (+10,980).
- Employment 0.5% of actual pay: 5,544 → 5,604 (+60).
- No income or resident tax at either salary. Premiums +17,088 on pay +10,000 → take-home falls by 7,088.
- Effective marginal rate = 1 + 7,088 / 10,000 = **1.7088**.

## Salary 1,050,000 → 1,060,000: joining employee insurance is *not* a wall for H1
H1 is single, so below the 88,000-a-month line they are not anyone's dependant: they pay National Health
Insurance (20,121) and the national pension (215,040) = 235,161. At 1,060,000 they pay employee premiums of
155,148 instead. Take-home rises from 814,839 to 904,852 (+90,013). The 1.06m and 1.3m walls hit people who
would otherwise be a **dependant** (for example H4's spouse), not single people.


## Revision 2026-11-03: the 88,000-a-month test ended on 2026-10-01 (persona review E3)
令和8年政令第275号 (sources/law/seirei_R8_275_shikoukijitsu.pdf) brought the abolition of the wage test into force on
2026-10-01; 日本年金機構's notice says 「賃金要件（所定内賃金が月額8.8万円以上）について、2026（令和8）年10月1日に撤廃されました」
(sources/social/nenkin_oshirase_20261001_chingin_yoken_teppai.html). So a part-timer working 20+ hours at a 51+ firm is in
employee insurance from October 2026 at any wage. Premiums count by month (国民年金法11条; 厚生年金保険法19条; NHI 月割).

Salary **1,050,000** (87,500 a month), single:
- January–September: NHI 20,121 × 9/12 = 15,090.75 → 15,090; national pension 17,920 × 9 = 161,280.
- October–December, standard monthly 88,000: health 4,334 × 3 = 13,002; child levy 101 × 3 = 303; pension 8,052 × 3 = 24,156;
  employment 0.5% × 87,500 = 437.5 → 437 × 3 = 1,311.
- Total 215,142. No income or resident tax (total income 310,000).
- Take-home = 1,050,000 − 215,142 = **834,858** (was 814,839).
- Salary 1,060,000 is in employee insurance all year, as before: 904,852. Step 1,050,000 → 1,060,000 = **+69,994** (was +90,013).
