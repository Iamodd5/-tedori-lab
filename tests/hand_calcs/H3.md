# H3 — dual-income couple, salaries 4,500,000 (35) and 3,000,000 (33), children 8 and 2

**Earner A (4,500,000).** Monthly 375,000 → band 370,000–395,000 → standard 380,000 (grade 26).
Health 18,715 × 12 = 224,580; levy 437 × 12 = 5,244; pension 34,770 × 12 = 417,240; employment 1,875 × 12 = 22,500 → **669,564**.
Employment income: salary 4,500,000 sits in the table band 4,500,000–4,504,000 → **3,160,000** (= 4,500,000 × 80% − 440,000).
Income tax: 3,160,000 − basic 1,040,000 − 669,564 = 1,450,436 → 1,450,000 × 5% = 72,500 × 1.021 = 74,022.5 → **74,000**.
(Spouse income 2,020,000 > 1,330,000 → no spouse deduction. Children under 16 → no dependant deduction.)
Resident: 3,160,000 − 430,000 − 669,564 = 2,060,436 → 2,060,000. 調整控除 base: taxable > 2m → max(50,000 − 60,000, 50,000) = 50,000. 特別区民税 2,060,000 × 6% = 123,600 − 1,500 = 122,100 + 3,000 = 125,100; 都民税 82,400 − 1,000 = 81,400 + 1,000 = 82,400; forest 1,000 → **208,500**.

**Earner B (3,000,000).** Monthly 250,000 → band 250,000–270,000 → standard 260,000 (grade 20).
Health 12,805 × 12 = 153,660; levy 299 × 12 = 3,588; pension 23,790 × 12 = 285,480; employment 1,250 × 12 = 15,000 → **457,728**.
Employment income: 3,000,000 × 70% − 80,000 = **2,020,000** (table band 3,000,000–3,004,000).
Income tax: 2,020,000 − 1,040,000 − 457,728 = 522,272 → 522,000 × 5% = 26,100 × 1.021 = 26,648.1 → **26,600**.
Resident: 2,020,000 − 430,000 − 457,728 = 1,132,272 → 1,132,000; 調整控除 base min(50,000, 1,132,000) = 50,000. 特別区民税 67,920 − 1,500 = 66,420 → 66,400 + 3,000 = 69,400; 都民税 45,280 − 1,000 = 44,280 → 44,200 + 1,000 = 45,200; forest 1,000 → **115,600**.

**Child allowance.** Age 8: 10,000; age 2: 15,000 → 25,000 × 12 = **300,000**.

**Disposable income** = 7,500,000 + 300,000 − (74,000 + 208,500 + 26,600 + 115,600) − (669,564 + 457,728) = **6,248,008**

**Revision 2026-11-03 (resident-tax rounding, OPEN_QUESTIONS Q14).** 地方税法20条の4の2第3項 rounds each tax's amount down to 100 yen, and 第8項 treats 特別区民税 and 都民税 as one tax only when a whole amount is under 100 yen (sources/law/chihozeiho_20270101.xml). So the income levy is worked out separately: 特別区民税 6% (314条の3) less 3% of the 調整控除 base (314条の6), and 都民税 4% (35条) less 2% (37条), each rounded down to 100 yen, then 均等割 3,000 + 1,000 and the forest environment tax 1,000. Ota City's worked example does the same (sources/tax/ota_keisan_no_shikumi.html: 201,540 → 201,500 and 133,360 → 133,300).
Before this revision earner B's levy was rounded once (110,700 + 5,000 = 115,700), and disposable income was 100 yen lower.
