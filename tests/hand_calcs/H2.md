## Student deferral income limit, revision 2026-11-03 (OPEN_QUESTIONS Q39)
国民年金法90条の3第1項第1号 and 施行令6条の9 (sources/law/kokumin_nenkin_sekourei_334CO0000000184.xml): a student may defer National Pension
only while their own income is at most 1,280,000 (no dependants). Income here (施行令6条の12) is 総所得金額 less the social-insurance
deduction and, for a working student, 270,000. In law it is the previous year's income and premiums; here the same year's, without the
pension premium itself.
- Salary **2,190,000**: total income 1,450,000; NHI (no reduction above 1,000,000): 47,600 + 76,602 + 17,600 + 28,560 + 1,873 + 2,754
  = 174,989. Test income 1,450,000 − 174,989 = 1,275,011 ≤ 1,280,000 → deferral, no national pension.
  Income tax: 1,450,000 − 1,040,000 − 174,989 = 235,011 → 235,000 × 5% = 11,750 × 1.021 = 11,996.75 → 11,900.
  Resident tax: 1,450,000 − 430,000 − 174,989 = 845,011 → 845,000; 50,700 − 1,500 = 49,200; 33,800 − 1,000 = 32,800; + 5,000 = 87,000.
  Take-home = 2,190,000 − 174,989 − 11,900 − 87,000 = **1,916,111**.
