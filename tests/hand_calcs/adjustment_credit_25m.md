# No resident-tax adjustment credit above total income of 25,000,000

地方税法314条の6 (ward) and 37条 (Tokyo): the 調整控除 applies only when 合計所得金額 is 25,000,000 or less.
Aged 45, salary 30,000,000 (employee): salary income 30,000,000 − 1,950,000 = 28,050,000; social insurance 1,839,468
(tested elsewhere); resident-tax basic deduction 0 (total income above 25,000,000).
- Taxable income 28,050,000 − 1,839,468 = 26,210,532 → 26,210,000.
- Ward 6% = 1,572,600; Tokyo 4% = 1,048,400; no 調整控除; flat 5,000. Resident tax **2,626,000**
  (the engine gave 2,623,500, with 1,500 + 1,000 of 調整控除).
