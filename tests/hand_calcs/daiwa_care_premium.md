# Daiwa plan: the 65+ care premium counts toward the cash limit for people who work

Daiwa (2025-10-10) p.4 図表3 (注1), as recorded in parameters/designs/daiwa.yaml `contributions_counted`: for people with
business or employment income, National Health Insurance, National Pension, long-term care (category 1) and late-elderly
premiums count. The category-1 care premium is the 65+ premium.

Aged 70, living alone, salary 1,000,000, not in company insurance (premiums from the 2026 rules, tested elsewhere):
- salary income 1,000,000 − 740,000 = 260,000; NHI 20,121; 65+ care premium band 1, 19,800 (measure 160,000 ≤ 826,500,
  nobody pays resident tax).
- Daiwa: basic deduction 0, so taxable income 260,000 − 20,121 − 19,800 = 220,079 → 220,000; tax 5% = 11,000; credit
  52,000; unused 41,000.
- Cash limit: 20,121 + 19,800 = 39,921, so cash **39,921** (with NHI alone it was 20,121).
